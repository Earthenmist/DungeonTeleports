# Mythic Dungeon Teleports - 2.2.0-Release

## Changes

- Added Party, Guild and Characters tabs to Keystones, with player counts, search, level filters and an option to hide entries without keys.
- Keystone rows now show dungeon icons, update times and teleport buttons. Refreshes preserve your scroll position and reuse existing rows.
- Updated window controls and Group Reminder to match the addon's class-colored theme.
- Group Reminder stays open on Escape, closes when you enter the destination instance, and supports manual closure or the close-on-teleport setting.
- Dungeon and raid names use Blizzard's localized names, with English fallbacks when unavailable.
- Simplified shared season data.

## Fixes

- Group Reminder identifies destinations by Blizzard map ID, avoiding mismatches caused by translated dungeon names.
- Corrected keystone dungeon identification and prevented older shared data from replacing newer updates.

## Known issues

- None known.
